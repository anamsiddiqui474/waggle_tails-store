OUR STORY

Made for the pets we couldn't shop for.

WaggleTails began because everything on the shelf was stiff, scratchy or built for someone else's pet. So we started making our own — soft-lined bands, rounded edges, no rough seams against the skin.

But it's about more than pets. Every collar is crocheted or stitched by skilled women working from their own homes across Pakistan — keeping traditional handcraft alive and turning it into a steady, dignified income on their own schedule.

When you buy a WaggleTails piece, your pet gets something soft and one-of-a-kind, and a maker at home gets paid fairly for her craft.
